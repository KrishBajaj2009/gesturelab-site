GETTING STARTED

Welcome to GestureLab

Trace a glowing shape. Your score combines how closely you stay near the guide and how much of its outline you cover.

  1. Try it now: Press and drag with your mouse, or draw using touch on a phone/tablet.
  2. Hands-free mode: Choose Camera, approve camera permission, then pinch your thumb and index finger together to draw.
  3. Calibrate: Pinch comfortably and press Calibrate pinch. Hold that gesture until calibration finishes.
  4. Release to score: Follow the complete outline, then release the pinch or pointer.

Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost. MediaPipe's model is downloaded when you first enable Camera. Your camera frames are processed in your browser; the third-party MediaPipe library may send usage metrics.

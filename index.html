QUICK START

Ready to draw?

Follow the outline to see how well you trace it. Your score combines precision and shape coverage.

  1. Pick a shape. Choose any of the four levels.
  2. Pointer mode: Press and drag using your mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen.
  3. Camera mode: Allow webcam access, then pinch your thumb and index finger to draw.
  4. Calibrate if needed: Pinch comfortably while using the Calibrate pinch button.
  5. Release to score. Check your result and try again.

Webcam access requires HTTPS or localhost. Gesture tracking uses Google's MediaPipe model in your browser.
